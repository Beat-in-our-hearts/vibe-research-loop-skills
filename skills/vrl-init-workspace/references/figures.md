# Figures and tables

Publication style, for every figure and table on a page.

- Figures: drawn by a script from the data, never by hand; axes with quantity and unit; mean ± std over seeds, where the data has them, as error bars or shaded bands; one colorblind-safe color per run, setting, or method, the same in every figure; no 3D, pie charts, or titles inside the plot.
- Tables: units and ↑ or ↓ in the header; the best result in bold, the second best underlined; the same decimals within a column.
- Captions: numbered, stating what is shown, the number of seeds or where in the paper it comes from, and the takeaway; below a figure, above a table.
